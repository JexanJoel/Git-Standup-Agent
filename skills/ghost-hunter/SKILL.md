# Ghost Hunter

## Instructions
Analyze reflog for lost commits.

Follow the **The Historian's Visual Standard (VISUAL_SPEC.md) v3.0** ONLY.

1. **Dashboard: Fragment Discovery**

2. **Section: Fragment List**
   - Use `[BOX]Lost Fragments | Table of discovered hashes and status badges...[/BOX]`
3. **Verdict**
   - `─── Verdict: [Your one-line insight] ───`
